# fluxboards for Windows

The whole bench on your PC. Free, with no limits.

**[Download for Windows](https://github.com/Vickyrrrrrr/fluxboards-desktop/releases/latest/download/Fluxboards-Setup.exe)** · Windows 10 & 11 · 64-bit

The Windows app is free. In its Local mode there are no limits on boards, routes, readiness checks or order files, and your boards stay on your PC. You sign in once; the AI runs on your own model key or coding agent, billed by your provider.

## New in 0.5.0

- Free, with no limits, in Local mode
- The latest tscircuit engine: routing 7% faster

## What it does

- **Your hardware.** Routing and every readiness check run on your own processor, with no queue.
- **Your agent.** An MCP server on your PC: Claude Code, Codex or Cursor designs, routes and checks boards with the app's own tools.
- **Mechanical CAD.** The agent models the enclosure and brackets around the board, checks them against it, and draws dimensioned PDF drawings.
- **Firmware on the board.** Run firmware on a twin of the board's microcontroller, wired to its netlist: RP2040, AVR, STM32 and nRF52.

## Install

1. Open `Fluxboards-Setup.exe`. It installs, then keeps itself up to date.
2. If Windows says it protected your PC, choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. The installer is not code-signed yet.
3. Sign in, then choose **Local**. Cloud opens your website boards instead.
